A Closer Look at our Trainings

HR Business Conduct Training

Our HR business conduct training can help your employees learn to handle a variety of situations effectively.

30% of workers do not know how to handle bad behavior. This training focuses on common workplace issues such as harassment, discrimination, and bullying. Studies show that this type of behavior can have profound psychological effects including decreased productivity on the victim, increased turnover, and even lawsuits.

Training Program Details

The program is divided into separate employee training modules*

  • Module 1 - Sexual Harassment, Hostile Work Environment
  • Module 2 - Sexual Orientation, Gender Identity, Quid Pro Quo
  • Module 3 - Bullying, How to Give Feedback
  • Module 4 - Gender Expression, Discrimination, Sexual Harassment

*Admin, HR, and manager trainings are also available.

Why Choose Sisu VR Trainings?

Virtual Reality Technology

Studies show virtual reality (VR) trainings raise retention and empathy. VR learners learn 4 times faster and are shown to be more confident at applying the skills learned.

20 Minute Modules

Each module (training course) is around 20 minutes long to help with retention and avoid fatigue. Short module times will guarantee that your employee’s get the training they need as efficiently as possible.

Employee Certificates

Each person that completes the training will get their own certificate with your company’s name on it. Certificates are proven to be a great way to encourage the completion of trainings.

Accessible Options

Although VR training has proven to be an excellent learning tool, we understand some might not prefer this method. Our trainings can be accessed through a computer and tablet.

Ethics Framework

Each training module will provide employee and employer with an ethical framework. Ethical framework allows trainees to see how they would naturally response to specific situations.

Company Portal

Monitor your employee’s progress and send reminders to complete training modules. Stay on top of trainings and help your company stay organized.
