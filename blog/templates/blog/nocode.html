Frequently Asked Questions

What game engines is this compatible with?

At the moment the Build Your Own Reality plugin is compatible with Unity, however we plan to support additional engines such as Unreal and Godot in the near future.

How can I customize my game? Can I add my own characters?

You can add your own character models, voice acting, animations, music etc.

What does "VR compatible" mean?

You can easily turn your story into a VR (virtual reality) experience, or keep it as a 2D desktop version.

Are there restrictions for the type of game possible with this plugin?

While the plugin provides the tools to make a simple point and click adventure or visual novel, there is no limit to the type of game an experience developer can make while utlizing this tool.
